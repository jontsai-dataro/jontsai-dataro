# Hi, I’m Jonathan Tsai — building at Dataro

I’m an engineering lead at [Dataro](https://dataro.io), where we build predictive AI for nonprofit fundraising.

Dataro helps fundraising teams turn donor data into clear, ranked actions: who to focus on, who to engage right now, and what to do next.

## Why Dataro

Nonprofits are asked to do more with less. The hard part is often decision debt: list building, exclusions, timing, prioritization, and repeating “what we did last year.”

Dataro sits on top of existing CRM data and helps teams make more focused campaign decisions with ranked priorities and practical next actions.
